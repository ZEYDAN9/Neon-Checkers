# Privacy Policy for Neon Checkers

Last updated: September 26, 2026

Daniel Tsaturian ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Neon Checkers mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and processed when you use the App.

## 1. Core Gameplay and Local Data

Neon Checkers does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number.

Core gameplay can operate offline.

Game settings, preferences, scores, saved matches, and similar gameplay information may be stored locally on your device using Apple's system storage. This information is used to provide App functionality and is not directly transmitted to us.

## 2. Advertising

Neon Checkers uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements.

The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect or process certain information, including:

- IP address, which may be used to estimate a device's general location;
- device identifiers;
- advertising data;
- app and advertisement interactions;
- diagnostic and performance information.

This information may be used by Google for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, and related advertising services.

For more information about Google's privacy practices, please visit:
https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 3. Advertising Consent and Privacy Choices

The App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to manage advertising consent and privacy choices where required by applicable laws and regulations.

Depending on your location and privacy choices, advertisements may be personalized, non-personalized, or otherwise limited in accordance with Google's advertising policies and applicable law.

Where required, the App provides a "Privacy Choices" option that allows you to review or update your advertising privacy preferences.

## 4. In-App Purchases

The App may offer in-app purchases, including an option to remove advertisements.

All purchases are processed securely by Apple through the App Store and StoreKit.

We do not receive or have access to your credit card number, payment card details, or Apple ID password. Apple may process transaction information according to Apple's own privacy policies.

## 5. Internet Connection

An internet connection may be required for advertisements, advertising consent messages, in-app purchases, purchase restoration, and related online services.

Core checkers gameplay does not require an internet connection.

## 6. Children's Privacy

Neon Checkers does not knowingly request or directly collect personal information from children through gameplay.

Third-party services used by the App may process information according to their own policies and applicable legal requirements.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
