# Privacy Policy for Neon Checkers
**Last updated: August 7, 2026**

Daniel Tsaturian ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Neon Checkers mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

## 1. Information Collection and Use
The App itself operates offline and does not require you to create an account, log in, or provide personal information such as your name, email, or phone number. We do not collect personal data directly through gameplay.

## 2. Third-Party Services and Advertising
We use third-party advertising services, specifically Google AdMob and its mediation partners (such as AppLovin), to support the App. These third-party services may collect and use certain data (such as IP address, device identifiers, and location data) to serve personalized advertisements.
* For more information on how Google uses data, please visit: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
* For AppLovin's Privacy Policy, please visit: [AppLovin Privacy](https://www.applovin.com/privacy/)

## 3. In-App Purchases
The App may offer in-app purchases. All transactions are securely processed through Apple's App Store. We do not process or have access to your payment information, credit card details, or Apple ID.
